How it works

Most reviews take about a week from first call to finished report.

  1. 30-minute call

    We talk through your setup and what’s worrying you. Free, no obligation.

  2. Read-only access

    You grant temporary read-only access. I never ask for passwords, and you can revoke access at any time.

  3. Report and fix plan

    You get the findings, the risk behind each one, and the steps to fix it.

  4. Fix it together

    Your team makes the changes with my guidance, or I make them for you.
